Import payslips

Pick one or more Concentrix payslip PDFs. The app fills in hours, night differential, overtime, leave, holiday pay, bonuses and deductions for each payday, and you check them before anything is saved. The app reads the PDF on this device and does not upload it. It never reads or keeps names or ID numbers. Set your hourly rate and allowances in Settings first so it can compare them.

The app downloads the PDF reader (pdf.js) from cdnjs.cloudflare.com the first time you import, so you need a connection then.

    SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG on this paydayFollows the payslip pattern: SSS every payday, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG on the first payday of the month, salary loan every payday.

    Built from one Concentrix employee’s Jul–Oct 2026 payslips, so treat the results as an estimate. SSS is 5% of that payday’s gross rounded to the nearest ₱500, and the slips were sometimes ₱25 higher. PhilHealth (2.5% of basic) and Pag-IBIG (2% up to ₱200 a month) are taken on the first payday of the month; the first-payday amounts are worked out from your year-to-date totals, not seen directly.
