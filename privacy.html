Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026 · Last updated: 13 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Payday Calculator handles information when you visit or use the website.

1. Who operates the website

Personal information controller / website operator: Schuyler Nikolai F. Licen, an individual operating Payday Calculator.

Privacy and support contact: skye07589@gmail.com.

This notice is intended to explain the website’s current data practices in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its applicable rules. The National Privacy Commission explains the right to be informed and the information a privacy notice should provide on its Right to Be Informed page.

2. Information you enter into the calculator

Depending on the features you use, information entered or saved in the calculator may include salary or hourly rates, pay dates, hours worked, overtime, night differential, allowances, deductions, loan amounts, and payroll figures extracted from a supported payslip PDF. These details may be personal information because they relate to an identifiable person.

3. Where calculator information is stored

The calculator saves your entries in your browser’s local storage on your device. The app does not send your salary entries, saved calculator records, or imported PDF files to the operator. The app does not have an account system or an operator-managed payroll database.

The PDF import feature reads the selected PDF in your browser. The app extracts supported payroll fields for you to review. The PDF file is not uploaded by the app. The current importer does not read or retain employee names or ID numbers.

Anyone with access to your browser profile or device may be able to access locally saved entries. Avoid using the calculator on a shared or public device. Use Reset all values in the app, or clear the website’s storage in your browser, to remove saved calculator entries.

4. External services and technical information

The website uses third-party services to operate:

  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website. When your browser requests website files, GitHub may process ordinary technical and connection information, such as IP address, browser details, and request information, under its own policies. See GitHub Pages information and GitHub’s Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts: the website requests font styles from Google. Google may receive technical request information when fonts load. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • cdnjs / Cloudflare: when you first import a PDF, the browser downloads the PDF.js library from cdnjs. The PDF contents are processed in your browser and are not sent to cdnjs by the app. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

These external requests mean the website does make network connections. The statement about local processing applies to your calculator entries and PDF contents, not to ordinary technical information involved in loading the website or third-party resources.

5. Purposes of processing

Your locally entered information is processed in your browser to calculate estimated pay, display pay periods and summaries, and save your settings and records for later visits. External services provide website hosting, font delivery, and the PDF-reading library.

6. Sharing and disclosure

The operator does not receive or disclose your payroll entries or imported PDF contents through the calculator. The third-party providers listed above receive technical information needed to deliver their services. They handle information under their own policies. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or a valid legal process.

7. Retention

Your calculator entries remain in browser storage until you reset them, clear the website’s storage, or remove the relevant browser data. The operator does not keep a separate copy of those entries. Third-party providers control retention of technical request data under their own policies.

8. Your privacy rights

Subject to the Data Privacy Act and its applicable rules, your rights may include the right to be informed, access, object, correct, request erasure or blocking, data portability, and lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. See the Commission’s Data Subject Rights page.

Because calculator entries stay in your browser and are not sent to the operator, you generally manage, correct, export, or delete those entries directly on your device. The operator does not have a copy to retrieve. For questions or a request relating to information the operator may hold, email skye07589@gmail.com. Please do not email payslips, passwords, or other sensitive information unless a secure method has been arranged.

9. Children

The calculator is a general payroll tool and is not designed to collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the operator, contact the email above.

10. Security

The website uses browser-side storage and processes imported PDFs in the browser. No internet-connected service is risk-free. Protect your device, browser profile, and access credentials. Keep your browser updated and avoid storing payroll details on a device shared with others.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website or its data practices change. The latest version and effective date will appear on this page. Material changes should be reviewed before continued use.
